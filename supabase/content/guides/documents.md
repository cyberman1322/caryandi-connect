**Check before you go:** requirements can change, so confirm the current list with the agency before each step. Always carry originals and keep copies of everything, including receipts.

## Importing a vehicle
| Stage | Documents | Who handles it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Export purchase and transit | Bill of Lading; commercial invoice; export certificate or Japanese deregistration document; JEVIC roadworthiness certificate; SAPS police clearance (South African imports) | Foreign supplier, JEVIC or QISJ, SAPS |
| 2. ZRA border customs | Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN); Form CE 20 customs entry declaration; purchase invoice; Bill of Lading; transit documents | ZRA Customs |
| 3. Police verification | ZRA Customs Clearance Certificate (CCC); NRC or passport; the vehicle itself; foreign deregistration certificate | Zambia Police Interpol unit |
| 4. RTSA registration | ZRA Customs Clearance Certificate; Interpol clearance certificate; RTSA physical inspection report; third-party insurance cover note; copy of your TPIN | RTSA |

## Buying a car that is already registered in Zambia
- The **Notification of Change of Ownership** form, completed and signed by the seller
- The **original registration book** (logbook)
- The **Interpol clearance** report from the police check of the chassis and engine numbers
- The **RTSA physical inspection report**
- A valid **third-party insurance** cover note in the buyer's name
- Copies of the buyer's **NRC** and **TPIN**
- Payment of the **K318.00** change-of-ownership fee

## Tips
- Make sure the names, chassis number and engine number are identical on every document. Mismatches are the most common reason a check fails.
- For imports, give your clearing agent the originals at least 10 days before the vehicle arrives at the port.
- Keep the ZRA Customs Clearance Certificate safe. RTSA and the police both ask for it, and you will need it if you sell the car later.
