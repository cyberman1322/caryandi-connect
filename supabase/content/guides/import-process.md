**Check before you pay:** this is a general guide compiled in September 2026. Procedures change, so confirm each step with the agency involved or a licensed clearing agent.

## The agencies involved
| Agency | Law or instrument | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA) | Customs and Excise Act (Cap 322); tariff books | Customs valuation, ASYCUDA processing, duty assessment, specific duty and carbon surtax collection |
| Road Transport and Safety Agency (RTSA) | Roads and Road Traffic Act; SI No. 25 of 2024 | Registration, fitness testing, registration books, road tax |
| Zambia Compulsory Standards Agency (ZCSA) | Compulsory Standards Act; standard ZS 560 | Pre-shipment roadworthiness checks and import quality standards at the border |
| Zambia Police Service (Interpol) | Zambia Police Act; SARPCCO protocols | Physical chassis and engine checks against stolen-vehicle databases; police clearance certificate |

## Stage 1: Purchase and export paperwork
1. Get the original **Bill of Lading** and **commercial invoice** from the seller.
2. Get the **export certificate** or Japanese **deregistration document**.
3. Complete the **JEVIC or QISJ pre-export inspection** (ZS 560 standard) before the vehicle ships. For South African purchases, get the **SAPS police clearance** instead.

## Stage 2: Transit port clearance and road transport
1. Appoint a transit clearing agent at the port (Dar es Salaam, Durban or Walvis Bay).
2. Lodge the **TRA transit bond** (Tanzania) or the **SARS transit bond** (South Africa).
3. Move the vehicle by road to the Zambian border post (Nakonde, Chirundu or Kazungula).

## Stage 3: ZRA customs at the border
1. Your agent lodges the customs declaration in ZRA's **ASYCUDA World** system.
2. Pay the ZRA duty (the fixed specific duty for used cars over 2 years old) and the **carbon emission surtax**.
3. Collect the **ZRA Customs Clearance Certificate (CCC)**.

## Stage 4: Zambia Police Interpol clearance
1. Take the vehicle to the Zambia Police motor-vehicle anti-theft bay.
2. Officers check the chassis and engine numbers against international databases.
3. Collect the **Interpol motor vehicle clearance certificate**.

## Stage 5: RTSA inspection, registration and licensing
1. The vehicle passes the **RTSA physical roadworthiness inspection**.
2. Get the **Certificate of Fitness** and **third-party insurance**.
3. Pay for the **registration book and number plates**.
4. Pay **road tax** for a quarter or a full year, based on the vehicle's gross weight.

## How long it takes
Allow about 4 to 7 weeks at sea from Japan, plus port clearance, road transport and the Zambian steps. Having your documents ready early, and lodging the transit bond before the ship arrives, is the best way to avoid port storage charges.
