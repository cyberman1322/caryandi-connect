**Check before you pay:** amounts are from the ZRA schedules as compiled in September 2026. ZRA revises duty regularly, so confirm the figure for your exact vehicle with ZRA or a licensed clearing agent before you commit to a purchase.

The Zambia Revenue Authority (**ZRA**) assesses duty at the border through its ASYCUDA World system. Which method applies depends mainly on the vehicle's age, then its body type, engine size and fuel type.

## New vehicles and vehicles under 2 years old: percentage (ad valorem) duty
New vehicles, vehicles less than 2 years old from manufacture, and low-emission or electric vehicles are taxed as a percentage of their **CIF value** (cost, insurance and freight).

### 1. Customs duty
| Body type | Rate |
|---|---|
| Sedans and hatchbacks | 25% of CIF |
| SUVs and station wagons | 30% of CIF |
| Pickups and light goods vehicles | 20% of CIF |
| Commercial trucks and heavy buses | 15% of CIF |

### 2. Excise duty
| Engine | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,500 cc | 20% |
| 1,501 – 2,500 cc | 30% |
| 2,501 – 3,000 cc | 40% |
| Over 3,000 cc | 50% |
| Hybrid vehicles | 15% (reduced rate) |
| Fully electric vehicles | 0% customs duty and 0% excise duty |

### 3. Import VAT
VAT at **16%** is charged on the CIF value plus customs duty plus excise duty, so the rates compound.

## Used vehicles over 2 years old: fixed (specific) duty
To avoid disputes over declared prices, ZRA charges used vehicles older than 2 years a **fixed Kwacha amount** from a schedule. The amount depends on body type, age band, engine size or weight, not on what you paid.

That single figure **already includes** customs duty, excise duty, import VAT, the motor vehicle fee, the ASYCUDA processing fee and surtax. The **Carbon Emission Surtax is not included** and is paid separately (see below).

Because the duty is the same whatever the car cost, a better-condition or lower-mileage car in the same band pays no more duty than a cheaper one.

## Carbon Emission Surtax
Charged on top of the duty above, based on engine size. It is paid at the border and renewed every year when you license the vehicle with RTSA.

| Engine | Surtax |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,500 cc | K123.20 |
| 1,501 – 2,000 cc | K246.40 |
| 2,001 – 3,000 cc | K352.00 |
| Over 3,000 cc | K484.00 |

## Worked example: two cars from Japan through Dar es Salaam
Both cars are over 5 years old. The exchange rate assumed is **USD 1 = K27.50**. These are illustrations, not quotes.

| Cost | Honda Fit (1,300 cc hatchback) | Toyota Hilux (2,700 cc double cab) |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase price in Japan (FOB) | USD 1,500 (~K41,250) | USD 12,000 (~K330,000) |
| JEVIC pre-export inspection | USD 350 (~K9,625) | USD 350 (~K9,625) |
| Sea freight and marine insurance | USD 1,200 (~K33,000) | USD 2,200 (~K60,500) |
| **CIF value at port of entry** | **USD 3,050 (~K83,875)** | **USD 14,550 (~K400,125)** |
| Tanzania port charges and TRA bond | USD 400 (~K11,000) | USD 400 (~K11,000) |
| Road transport to the border | K3,500 | K5,000 |
| ZRA specific duty | K29,491.36 | K63,225.25 |
| Carbon emission surtax | K123.20 | K352.00 |
| Clearing agent fee | K3,000 | K4,000 |
| Zambia Police Interpol inspection | K166.80 | K166.80 |
| RTSA inspection and Certificate of Fitness | K120.80 | K120.80 |
| RTSA registration book and plates | K836.00 | K836.00 |
| First year of road tax | K587.00 | K807.00 |
| Third-party insurance (estimate) | K1,000 | K1,500 |
| **Estimated total, landed and registered** | **about K133,700** | **about K487,130** |

Storage charges, agent acquittal fees and exchange-rate movements can add to these totals. See the imports guide.
