**Check before you pay:** the fees below come from Statutory Instrument No. 25 of 2024 as compiled in September 2026. RTSA can change them, so confirm the amount at an RTSA office or on ZamPortal before you pay.

## Who does what
The Road Transport and Safety Agency (**RTSA**) registers vehicles, carries out physical roadworthiness inspections, issues Certificates of Fitness and number plates, keeps the national vehicle register and collects road tax. The Zambia Police Service **Interpol** unit checks the chassis and engine numbers against stolen-vehicle databases before a vehicle is registered or transferred.

## Buying or selling a car that is already registered in Zambia
1. **The seller notifies RTSA.** The seller completes and signs the RTSA **Notification of Change of Ownership** form. This releases the seller from liability for the vehicle. If RTSA is not notified, the vehicle can be impounded during road checks.
2. **Police verification.** The buyer takes the vehicle to the Zambia Police Interpol unit. Officers confirm that the chassis and engine numbers match the registration book and issue a clearance report.
3. **RTSA inspection.** The vehicle is inspected at RTSA (K46.80) and an updated inspection report is issued.
4. **New registration book.** The buyer presents the inspection report, the Interpol clearance, the original registration book, a valid third-party insurance cover note and copies of their NRC and TPIN. After paying the **K318.00** change-of-ownership fee, RTSA cancels the old logbook and issues a new one in the buyer's name.

Property Transfer Tax does not apply to motor vehicles. A vehicle transfer only costs the RTSA and police fees above.

## Tips
- Don't hand over the full payment until the seller has signed the change-of-ownership notification and given you the original registration book.
- Make sure the chassis and engine numbers on the car match the logbook before you pay. The Interpol check will fail if they don't.
- Finish the transfer promptly. Driving a vehicle that has not been transferred risks impoundment.
